## The packaging decision

I started the mount design after we got the off-the-shelf gearbox shifting out of neutral. The engine, gearbox, and CVT casing already sit close together in our vehicle assembly. Moving one changes the space the others can use.

Lowering the engine could lower the vehicle’s center of gravity. Moving the gearbox rearward could create room for that change, but it would also move the driveline away from the geometry the dynamics team has been using. It could leave part of the gearbox outside the frame. I raised both concerns during the layout review.

## Design ownership

My part is the mount for the off-the-shelf gearbox and its fit in the vehicle. Other members of our group are developing gearbox concepts. I asked them to document the ratio, gear arrangement, module, and material they considered, along with the reasons for each choice.

## Next checks

I’m reviewing the mount position against the engine, CVT casing, and frame before we lock the CAD. The next step is to compare layouts with the dynamics team and review material and load assumptions with Simulation & Validation. Physical fit-up and test results are still pending for this mount.
