Project

For ME 374C, Combustion Engine Processes, our team studied how intake geometry affects small-engine performance. We focused on measuring what changed when we revised the intake geometry.

Test setup

We built and validated a dynamometer, then used it to collect performance data and compare the redesigned intake against the baseline setup.

Results

The redesigned geometry produced a measurable gain in brake power. We presented the setup and findings at UT Austin’s Senior Capstone Design Day.
